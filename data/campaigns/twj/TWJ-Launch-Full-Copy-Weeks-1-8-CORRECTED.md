## IMAGE PLAN FOR FACEBOOK POSTS

17 total Facebook posts across the pipeline (16 across Weeks 1–8, plus 1 on Day 3). Rather than a unique image per post, these map to a small set of reusable templates Watson can composite from elements you provide.

| Template Type | Used By | Elements Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Cover + tagline card | Wk1 Post1, Wk8 Post1 | Book cover, wordmark/logo, background |
| Quote card | Wk2 Post2, Wk3 Post1, Wk6 Post1, Wk7 Post1, Day3 Post | Background/texture, author mark, font treatment |
| Personal/behind-the-scenes card | Wk1 Post2, Wk3 Post2 | Author photo (optional) or simple text background |
| Problem/tension card | Wk2 Post1 | Quote card template, darker/serious background variant |
| Date/countdown card | Wk4 Post2, Wk6 Post2 | "Date" background element, numeral treatment |
| Benefit/CTA card | Wk4 Post1, Wk5 Post2, Wk7 Post2, Wk8 Post1 | Cover image + button/link graphic |
| Testimonial card | Wk5 Post1, Wk7 Post1 | Quote card + quotation-mark graphic |
| Gratitude/thank-you card | Wk8 Post2 | Cover image + warm background, space for names |

**Elements to create and drop in the folder for Watson:**
1. Book cover (have it — `wrong-jesus-cover-iso.png`)
2. 2–3 background textures/colors (neutral, serious/dark, warm)
3. Wordmark or logo mark
4. Quotation mark graphic element
5. Simple "buy now" / arrow / button graphic
6. Optional: one author photo

Flag this list to the development project so Claude Code knows what compositing logic to build (text-over-background generation per post, not one-off custom images).

### Brevo Email — "A story from the book"
Subject: A story from the book

I want to tell you the story that started all of this.

I was preparing a Palm Sunday sermon — the kind I'd preached a dozen times before — when something about the crowd stopped me. They weren't half-hearted. They were fully, loudly, physically worshiping. Branches in the air, coats on the ground, voices raised.

And within days, that same passion had nowhere to land, because the Jesus they'd built in their minds wasn't the Jesus who was actually there.

That's when it hit me: my own passion in worship doesn't prove I've got it right. It only proves I mean it. Meaning it and getting it right are two different things.

That question is what *The Wrong Jesus* is about. Launch day is coming — I'll have the date for you soon.
