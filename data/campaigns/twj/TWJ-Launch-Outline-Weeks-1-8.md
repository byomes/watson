# The Wrong Jesus — 8-Week Launch Playbook: Structural Outline

*Reference sample for future book launches. Structure follows the WCKY Book Launch Framework; hooks are specific to The Wrong Jesus (Palm Sunday moment, passion vs. accuracy, sincere-believer audience).*

**Core hooks used throughout:**
1. Central premise: it is possible to passionately worship the wrong Jesus — the question is whether we worship him through our own lens or on his terms.
2. Personal story: William's Palm Sunday sermon-prep moment — the crowd's passionate worship of Jesus was still wrong about who he was.
3. Key line: "The passion of your worship does not generate its accuracy — only its authenticity."
4. Target audience: not skeptics, but sincere, ardent believers who assume their view of Jesus is correct and have never checked.

---

## Week 1 — SEED
- FB Post 1: Personal announcement, book exists, teases core question, no links
- FB Post 2: Behind the book — the Palm Sunday moment, first person
- Kit Email: "Something I've been working on" — intro + ARC recruitment CTA
- Givebutter Email: "A gift for you when the book drops" — signed copy benefit, gratitude, no ask
- ARC: recruitment open via /arc page

## Week 2 — BUILD
- FB Post 1: The problem — sincere believers who've never verified their lens
- FB Post 2: Pulled quote — passion vs. accuracy tension
- Kit Email: "The question this book answers" — own lens vs. his terms; late ARC signups

## Week 3 — DEEPEN
- FB Post 1: The Palm Sunday story, illustrative and human
- FB Post 2: Why this matters for ordinary believers, not scholars
- Kit Email: Expanded Palm Sunday story, soft tease of launch date
- Givebutter Email: East Africa field update + signed copy benefit reminder

## Week 4 — ARC CLOSE + PIVOT TO LAUNCH
- FB Post 1: ARC close announcement, last call for review copies
- FB Post 2: Launch date reveal, clean and clear
- Kit Email: "The date is set" — clear CTA
- ARC Reader Email: access closes launch day, request reviews (Amazon/Goodreads), soft one-line FMS invitation

## Week 5 — MOMENTUM
- FB Post 1: Early reader feedback (with permission)
- FB Post 2: Signed copy benefit reminder, soft new donor recruitment
- Kit Email: "What people are saying" — 2-3 reader reactions
- Givebutter Email: "One week out" — shipping address confirmation, gratitude

## Week 6 — MOMENTUM CONTINUED / BRIDGE
- FB Post 1: Deeper dive into core argument — short excerpt on worship "on his terms"
- FB Post 2: Countdown post, anticipation without over-hype
- Kit Email: "Almost here" — restate hook, drive to launch day

## Week 7 — FINAL APPROACH
- FB Post 1: Final ARC testimonials, social proof
- FB Post 2: Logistics — where to buy, how to become an FMS partner for signed copy
- Kit Email: "This time next week" — anticipation, set expectations
- Givebutter Email: Final shipping address confirmation call

## Week 8 — LAUNCH
- FB Post 1 (morning): It is here — clean image, purchase link, FMS CTA
- FB Post 2 (evening): Thank you post, name ARC readers publicly (with permission)
- Kit Email: "It is live" — purchase link + signed copy partner invitation
- Givebutter Email: "Your signed copy is on its way" — gratitude, East Africa impact
- ARC Reader Email: Launch day thank you, review reminder, one-line FMS invitation

---

*Note: This is an 8-week expansion of the original 6-week framework, adding a Week 6 momentum/bridge beat and extending final approach into Week 7. For future launches, keep this cadence and rewrite the hooks (Week 2-3 problem/story, Week 5-7 momentum) around the new book's own sharpest strengths.*
