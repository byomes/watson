## Main Resource Categories

**Algorithmic Trading**: Free introductory guide ("A Rough and Ready Guide") covering principles, practices, components of algorithmic trading and career pathways; free blogs on financial markets, trading fundamentals, algo trading in India, career guidance.

**Python for Trading**: Free course on stock data import/manipulation/visualization; Python basics handbook; tutorials on data structures, Jupyter notebooks, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Plotly, Scikit-learn.

**Machine Learning**: Free course on ML algorithms and research papers; free eBook on neural networks in Python; tutorials on ML basics, deep learning, NLP applications for trading.

**Options Trading**: Free course covering covered calls, protective puts, iron condors, spreads; tutorial on terminology, Greeks, Black-Scholes model; 12 strategy guides (diagonal spreads, calendar spreads, straddles, collars, iron butterflies).

**Risk Management & Strategic Trading**:
- Mean Reversion & Statistical Arbitrage — blog tags and course recommendations
- Momentum Trading — free course on momentum strategies with backtesting and risk management

**Data Science**: Project-based courses (English Premier League predictions, food delivery app case study).

**Quantitative Trading (Quants)**: Quant interview prep (logic puzzles, statistics, probability, time series, portfolio management, ML); statistics/probability distribution guide; salary/career guidance.

**Forex Trading**: Basics of forex trading; algorithmic trading in FX markets (HFT).

**Automated Trading**: Course on Interactive Brokers/IBridgePy platform; automated trading systems tutorial covering architecture, protocols, low-latency systems, and **backtesting bias awareness**; Blueshift — free backtesting platform supporting multiple asset classes (FX, equities, futures).
